Think Fast!

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

In short: Think Fast! does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your progress lives on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Think Fast! is a memory game for iPhone and iPad, developed and published by Anas Tabit (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, does not ask for your name, email or location, and does not send any information to us or to any server we operate.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game between sessions, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Game progress, scores, records and achievements
  • Your coin balance, lives and unlocked items
  • Settings such as language, sound and appearance

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple services the app uses

Some features rely on Apple’s own services. Apple handles that information under the Apple Privacy Policy, not this one.

  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. Leaderboards show your Game Center nickname. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device’s Settings.
  • In-app purchases and subscriptions. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, card or payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, so it can unlock it. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device’s Settings under your Apple Account.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example when your lives have refilled or a new daily challenge is ready). They are created on the device itself and involve no data sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • Crash reports. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports. These do not identify you, and you can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All game data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. For data held by Apple, such as Game Center or purchase history, you can manage it through your Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling personal data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page at github.com/tabitanas/thinkfast/issues.
